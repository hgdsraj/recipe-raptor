Pulled sous vide chicken (see other note)
Ingredients:


2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts
1 tablespoon olive oil
2 teaspoons ground cumin
2 teaspoons chili powder
1 teaspoon smoked paprika
1 teaspoon garlic powder
1 teaspoon onion powder
1 teaspoon salt
1/2 teaspoon black pepper
Instructions:


Preheat your sous vide water bath to 150°F (66°C).


In a small bowl, mix together the olive oil, cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and black pepper.


Rub the spice mixture all over the chicken breasts.


Place the chicken breasts in a vacuum-sealed bag or a resealable freezer bag, removing as much air as possible before sealing.


Place the bagged chicken breasts in the sous vide water bath and cook for 2-3 hours.


Once the cooking time is up, remove the chicken breasts from the water bath and let them rest for 10-15 minutes.


Open the bag and remove the chicken breasts. Reserve the juices for later.


Shred the chicken using two forks or a stand mixer fitted with a paddle attachment.


Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the shredded chicken and some of the reserved juices. And oil. Cook until the chicken is heated through and slightly crispy on the outside.


Serve the shredded chicken in a burrito with your favorite toppings.


Note: The cooking time and temperature may vary depending on the thickness of the chicken breasts, so it's important to use a meat thermometer to ensure that the internal temperature of the chicken reaches at least 165°F (74°C) for food safety.

Teriyaki brocolli and tofu stir fry (see note)
Teriyaki Tofu and Broccoli Stir Fry:


Ingredients:


1 block of firm tofu, pressed and cubed
1 head of broccoli, chopped into florets
1 red bell pepper, sliced
1/2 cup of teriyaki sauce
2 tablespoons of sesame oil
2 cloves of garlic, minced
1 tablespoon of fresh ginger, grated
Salt and pepper to taste
Cooked rice, for serving
Instructions:


Heat up the sesame oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat.
Add in the cubed tofu and stir fry for 5-6 minutes or until it is browned and slightly crispy.
Remove the tofu from the skillet and set aside.
In the same skillet, add in the chopped broccoli and sliced red bell pepper. Stir fry for 3-4 minutes or until the vegetables are tender-crisp.
Add the garlic and ginger to the skillet and cook for 1 minute or until fragrant.
Add the teriyaki sauce to the skillet and stir to combine with the vegetables.
Return the tofu to the skillet and toss to coat with the teriyaki sauce.
Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes or until the sauce has thickened and the tofu is heated through.
Season with salt and pepper to taste.
Serve over cooked rice.

Pizza Dough
Ingredients:


1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
1 tsp instant yeast
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp sugar
3/4 cup warm water
1 tbsp olive oil
Instructions:


In the bowl of your stand mixer, whisk together the flour, instant yeast, salt, and sugar.
Attach the dough hook to your mixer and turn it on low speed.
Slowly pour in the warm water and olive oil, and let the mixer knead the dough for about 5-7 minutes until it forms a smooth, elastic ball of dough.
Remove the dough from the mixer bowl and form it into a ball.
Place the dough ball in a lightly greased bowl, cover with a clean kitchen towel, and let it rise in a warm place for about 30-45 minutes, or until it has doubled in size.
Once the dough has risen, punch it down to release any air bubbles and roll it out into a circle or rectangle, depending on your pizza pan or preference.
Top the pizza dough with your favorite toppings and bake in a preheated 425°F (218°C) oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and crispy.

Sure, here's a recipe for super creamy and delicious baked russet potato cubes that are also healthy!


Ingredients:


4 medium russet potatoes, scrubbed and cubed
2 tablespoons olive oil
1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
1/2 teaspoon onion powder
1/2 teaspoon paprika
1/2 teaspoon salt
1/4 teaspoon black pepper
1/2 cup plain Greek yogurt
1/2 cup low-fat milk
1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
Chopped fresh chives or parsley for garnish (optional)
Instructions:


Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C).
In a large bowl, toss the cubed potatoes with olive oil, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper until evenly coated.
Transfer the seasoned potatoes to a large baking sheet and spread them out in a single layer.
Bake the potatoes for 25-30 minutes, or until they are golden brown and tender.
Meanwhile, in a small saucepan, heat the Greek yogurt, low-fat milk, and Parmesan cheese over low heat, stirring constantly, until the cheese is melted and the mixture is smooth.
Once the potatoes are baked, remove them from the oven and transfer them to a large bowl.
Pour the creamy yogurt sauce over the potatoes and toss gently to coat.
Serve the potatoes hot, garnished with chopped chives or parsley if desired.
This recipe yields about 4 servings. Enjoy your super creamy and super yummy baked russet potato cubes!


Crispy wafer GPT


To make a delicious, light, and crispy wafer layer cookie, you will need the following ingredients and steps:


Ingredients:


1 cup all-purpose flour
1/2 cup granulated sugar
1/2 cup unsalted butter, at room temperature
1 large egg, at room temperature
1 tsp. vanilla extract
1/4 tsp. baking powder
1/4 tsp. salt
2 tbsp. whole milk
Steps:


Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C) and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.


In a medium-sized mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until well combined.


In a separate large mixing bowl, use an electric mixer to cream together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy, about 3-4 minutes.


Add the egg and vanilla extract to the butter mixture, and beat until well combined.


Slowly add the flour mixture to the butter mixture, mixing on low speed until just combined.


Add the milk to the batter and beat until well combined.


Using a tablespoon, scoop the batter onto the lined baking sheet, spacing them about 2 inches apart.


Use the back of the spoon to spread the batter into thin, even circles.


Bake for 8-10 minutes, or until the edges of the cookies are golden brown.


Remove from the oven and let cool on the baking sheet for 5 minutes.


Carefully transfer the cookies to a wire rack to cool completely.


Optional: You can sandwich two cookies together with your favorite filling (e.g. Nutella, whipped cream, jam, etc.) to make a delicious wafer cookie sandwich.


Enjoy your delicious, light, and crispy wafer layer cookies!


Tortillas


Ingredients
3 cups all-purpose flour
1 teaspoon salt
1 teaspoon baking powder
⅓ cup extra virgin olive oil, vegetable oil or other fairly neutral flavored oil
1 cup warm water
Instructions
To make by hand:
Combine flour, salt and baking powder in a medium-size bowl. Using a sturdy silicone spatuala or a sturdy wooden spoon, mix dry ingredients until well combined.


Make a well in the center of the dry ingredients and add the oil and water. Stir well from the bottom up, until all dry ingredients are incorporated and the dough begins to come together and form a shaggy ball.


Turn dough out onto a lightly floured work surface and knead for 1-2 minutes until the dough is nice and smooth. Proceed with step number 3 below for the remainder of the recipe.


To make with a mixer:
Combine flour, salt and baking powder in the bowl of a stand mixer. Using the dough hook, mix dry ingredients until well combined.


Add oil and water with mixer running at a medium speed. After about 1 minute, or when mixture comes together and begins to form a ball, decrease mixing speed to low. Continue to mix for 1 minute or until dough is smooth.


Transfer dough to a lightly floured work surface. Divide into 16 equal portions. Turn each piece to coat with flour. Form each piece into a ball and flatten with the palm of your hand. Cover flattened balls of dough with a clean kitchen towel and allow to rest for at least 15 minutes (or as much as 2 hours) before proceeding.


After the rest period, heat a large pan over medium heat. Roll each dough piece into a rough circle, about 6-7 inches in diameter, keep work surface and rolling pin lightly floured. Don’t stack uncooked tortillas on top of each other or they will stick together. (I like to separate my tortillas with parchement paper.)


When the pan is hot, place one dough circle into the pan and allow to cook 45 seconds to 1 minute or until the bottom surface has a few pale brown spots and the uncooked surface is bubbly. If browning too fast, reduce the heat a bit. If it’s taking longer than a minute to see a few pale golden brown spots on the underside of tortillas, increase the heat a bit. Flip to other side and cook for 15-20 seconds. The tortillas should be nice and soft but have a few small brown spots on the surface. 


Remove from pan with tongs and stack in a covered container or zippered bag to keep the tortillas soft. 


Serve warm or allow to cool for later use. When ready to use, place a slightly damp paper towel in the bottom of a microwave-safe container (with a cover) that will hold the stacked tortillas. Microwave uncovered for 15-30 seconds (start with 15) or until warm, then keep covered to hold heat while serving.


Store in an airtight container or zippered bag at room temperature for 24 hours or refrigerate for up to 1 week. To freeze, separate tortillas with parchment paper or waxed paper and place in a zippered bag before placing in the freezer.

Bake dfish and chips
Sure, here's a recipe for baked fish and chips that serves 4 people:


Ingredients for the fish:


4 fillets of white fish (such as cod or haddock)
1 cup of all-purpose flour
1 teaspoon of baking powder
1/2 teaspoon of salt
1/2 teaspoon of black pepper
1/2 teaspoon of paprika
1/2 teaspoon of garlic powder
1/2 teaspoon of onion powder
1/2 cup of milk
1 egg
1 cup of breadcrumbs
Cooking spray
Ingredients for the chips:


4 medium-sized potatoes, peeled and cut into thick wedges
1 tablespoon of olive oil
Salt and pepper to taste
Instructions:


Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.


Prepare the fish: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk and egg.


Dip each fillet into the flour mixture, then into the milk mixture, and finally coat with the breadcrumbs. Place the fish fillets on the prepared baking sheet.


Spray the tops of the fish fillets with cooking spray and bake in the preheated oven for 20-25 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.


While the fish is baking, prepare the chips: Toss the potato wedges with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Place them on a separate baking sheet and bake in the oven for 25-30 minutes, or until golden brown and crispy.


Serve the fish and chips hot with your favorite dipping sauce, such as tartar sauce or ketchup.


Enjoy your delicious and healthier version of fish and chip




Tartar sauce


Sure, here's a recipe for a healthy tartar sauce that you can use with your baked fish and chips:


Ingredients:


1/2 cup of plain Greek yogurt
1/4 cup of diced pickles
1 tablespoon of capers, drained and finely chopped
1 tablespoon of fresh lemon juice
1 tablespoon of chopped fresh dill
1 teaspoon of Dijon mustard
Salt and pepper to taste
Instructions:


In a small bowl, stir together the Greek yogurt, pickles, capers, lemon juice, dill, and mustard.


Season the tartar sauce with salt and pepper to taste.


Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes to allow the flavors to meld.


Serve the tartar sauce cold with your baked fish and chips.


This healthy tartar sauce recipe is a great alternative to traditional tartar sauce, which is typically made with mayonnaise and can be high in calories and fat. Greek yogurt is a great source of protein and calcium, and using it in place of mayonnaise makes this tartar sauce much lighter and healthier. The addition of pickles, capers, and fresh herbs adds lots of flavor without adding extra calories or fat.

Here is a basic recipe for romesco sauce:


Ingredients:
- 2 red bell peppers, roasted and peeled
- 1/2 cup blanched almonds
- 1/4 cup breadcrumbs
- 2 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1 tablespoon sherry vinegar
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste


Instructions:
1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C). 
2. Cut the red bell peppers in half, remove the stem and seeds, and place them on a baking sheet cut-side down. 
3. Roast the peppers in the oven for 20-25 minutes, or until the skin is charred and blistered. 
4. Remove the peppers from the oven and place them in a bowl. Cover the bowl with plastic wrap and let the peppers cool for 10-15 minutes. 
5. Once the peppers are cool, remove the skin and discard it. 
6. In a food processor or blender, combine the roasted red peppers, blanched almonds, breadcrumbs, garlic, and sherry vinegar. 
7. Process the mixture until it is smooth and well combined. 
8. Slowly drizzle in the olive oil while the blender or food processor is running, and continue to process the mixture until it is thick and creamy. 
9. Season the romesco sauce with salt and pepper to taste. 


And to turn the sauce into romesco jus, simply add chicken or vegetable broth to the sauce and blend it together until it reaches your desired consistency. You can adjust the amount of broth to create a thinner or thicker jus, depending on your preference. The jus can be heated in a saucepan on the stove or in the microwave, and served warm over meat, fish, or vegetables.

Fresh pesto zuchinni linguine 




Ingredients:
- 8 ounces linguine
- 2 medium zucchini
- 1 cup fresh basil leaves
- 1/4 cup pine nuts
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Optional toppings: cherry tomatoes, red pepper flakes, additional Parmesan cheese


Instructions:


1. Cook the linguine according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.


2. While the linguine is cooking, prepare the zucchini. Trim off the ends and cut the zucchini into thin, long strips resembling linguine using a spiralizer or a vegetable peeler. Set aside.


3. In a food processor or blender, combine the basil leaves, pine nuts, minced garlic, and grated Parmesan cheese. Pulse until the ingredients are finely chopped.


4. With the food processor or blender running, slowly drizzle in the olive oil until the mixture forms a smooth and creamy pesto sauce. Add salt and pepper to taste, and blend again to combine.


5. In a large skillet, heat a drizzle of olive oil over medium heat. Add the zucchini noodles and sauté for 2-3 minutes until they are just tender. Be careful not to overcook them, as they can become mushy.


6. Add the cooked linguine to the skillet with the zucchini noodles. Pour the fresh pesto sauce over the noodles and gently toss to coat everything evenly. Cook for an additional 1-2 minutes until the sauce is heated through.


7. Remove from heat and serve the pesto zucchini linguine immediately. Optionally, you can garnish with halved cherry tomatoes, red pepper flakes for a hint of spice, and additional grated Parmesan cheese.


Enjoy your fresh pesto zucchini linguine!

Yes! Japanese lamb curry with potatoes is a delicious and comforting twist on the classic **Japanese kare raisu (カレーライス)** — usually made with beef or pork, but lamb works beautifully. It’s savory, slightly sweet, thick, and mild — perfect with rice.


You can use store-bought **Japanese curry roux blocks** (like Golden Curry, Vermont Curry, or Java Curry), or make a quick homemade version if you don’t have them. I’ll include both options.


---


## 🇯🇵 **Japanese Lamb Curry with Potatoes (カレーライス風ラムカレー)**


### 🍛 Serves 4 | Comforting, mild, rich — perfect over white rice


---


### **Ingredients**


* 3 lbs **cubed lamb leg**
* Salt & pepper
* 1 tbsp **oil** (neutral or olive oil)
* 1 large **onion**, sliced
* 3 **carrots**, peeled and chopped
* 5 medium **potatoes**, peeled and cut into chunks
* 1 clove **garlic**, minced
* 1 tsp **ginger**, grated or minced
* 3 cups ** chicken broth**
Honey
Mirin
Dark souls sauce
Rice wine vinegar


Serve with green onion
Pickled onion




#### Option A: Use store-bought curry roux


* ½ box (about 100g) of **Japanese curry roux blocks** (Golden, Vermont, etc.)


#### Option B: Homemade curry base (if no roux)


* 2 tbsp **flour**
* 1 tbsp **curry powder** (Japanese or mild)
* 1 tbsp **soy sauce**
* 1 tbsp **ketchup**
* 1 tsp **Worcestershire sauce**
* 1 tsp **sugar**
* Optional: 1 tsp **garam masala** for warmth


---


### **Instructions**


#### 1. Sear the lamb


* Season lamb with salt and pepper.
* In a **Dutch oven** or pot, heat oil over medium-high. Sear lamb cubes until browned on all sides (6–8 min). Set aside.


#### 2. Sauté aromatics & veggies


* In the same pot, add onion. Cook until softened and starting to brown (6–8 min).
* Add garlic and ginger. Cook for 1 min.
* Add carrots and potatoes, and stir for another 2 min.


#### 3. Add liquids


* Add the seared lamb back to the pot.
* Pour in 3 cups of water or broth.
* Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
* Skim off foam if needed. Cover and cook for **1.5 hours**, or until lamb is tender. (Or transfer to oven at 325°F/163°C.)


#### 4. Add curry roux


* If using **store-bought roux**: Break up the curry blocks and stir them in. Simmer uncovered for another **10–15 min**, until thick and glossy.
* If using **homemade**: In a small pan, heat 1 tbsp oil or butter. Add flour and stir until light brown. Add curry powder, soy sauce, ketchup, Worcestershire, sugar. Mix well and add to the pot. Stir until thickened.


#### 5. Serve


* Serve over **steamed Japanese rice**.
* Optional toppings: **pickled ginger (fukujinzuke)**, boiled eggs, or green peas.

Here’s an elite-level **Tori Paitan Ramen** recipe — the kind you’d find at a top Tokyo ramen-ya. Rich, creamy, and deeply chicken-forward without being greasy.


---


### 🕕 Total time


6–7 hours (mostly simmering)
Makes 4 servings


---


## 🐔 Broth (the heart of it)


**Ingredients**


* 3 lbs (1.4 kg) chicken carcasses and wings (necks optional)
* 1 lb (450 g) chicken feet (crucial for collagen richness)
* 1 whole chicken breast or thigh (for extra flavor)
* 1 leek (roughly chopped)
* 1 onion (quartered)
* 1 knob ginger (smashed)
* 6 cloves garlic (smashed)
* 1 sheet kombu (optional, 10 cm)
* Water (enough to cover, about 4–5 L)


**Steps**


1. **Parboil the bones:**
   Add all chicken parts to a large pot, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Boil 5 minutes. Dump everything into a colander and rinse bones under cold water.
   *→ Removes scum and blood = clean flavor, pure white broth.*


2. **Hard simmer:**
   Return the bones to the pot, cover with fresh water. Bring to a *rolling boil*, not a gentle simmer — this agitation helps emulsify fat and collagen into a milky white broth.
   Skim foam for the first 10 minutes, then don’t worry about clarity.


3. **Add aromatics:**
   Add leek, onion, ginger, garlic, and kombu (if using). Continue hard simmering for **5–6 hours**, topping up with hot water as it reduces.


4. **Blend (optional but key for creamy texture):**
   Toward the end, remove large solids, then ladle some broth with soft chicken bits into a blender. Blend until smooth and creamy, then return to the pot.
   *→ This gives that velvety “paitan” thickness.*


5. **Strain:**
   Pass through a fine sieve. Keep hot or chill immediately.


---


## 🍜 Tare (seasoning base)


**Shio Tare (simple salt style):**


* 3 tbsp fine sea salt
* 2 tbsp mirin
* 1 tbsp sake
* 1 tbsp dried anchovy powder (optional but amazing)
* 1 tbsp chicken fat (or neutral oil)
* 100 ml chicken broth (reduce from your main pot)


Simmer gently for 5 minutes to meld, then cool.


---


## 🧄 Aromatic Oil (optional but powerful)


**Chicken oil or garlic oil:**


* Render chicken skin slowly over low heat until crisp and golden.
* Add a few slices of garlic and a small piece of ginger to perfume the oil.
* Strain and store.


---


## 🍜 Ramen Assembly


**Per bowl:**


* 300 ml tori paitan broth (hot)
* 1 tbsp shio tare (adjust to taste)
* 1 tsp aromatic oil
* Ramen noodles (fresh, medium thickness)
* Toppings:


  * Sliced chicken chashu (poached thigh or breast)
  * Ajitsuke tamago (marinated egg)
  * Menma (bamboo shoots)
  * Scallions or negi oil drizzle
  * Nori or mizuna (optional)


**To serve:**


1. In your bowl, add tare and oil.
2. Pour in the hot broth and stir.
3. Add cooked noodles, top neatly.


---


### 🔥 Pro tips from ramen pros


* **Boil vigorously** the entire time — unlike clear broth. Cloudiness is good.
* **Chicken feet = collagen = mouthfeel.**
* **Blend the broth** for the perfect emulsion — don’t skip this.
* Make **shio tare fresh** and adjust saltiness only at serving.
* Let the **broth rest overnight** in the fridge for deeper umami.
